# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

ABL ("ABL", "we", "us") is a personal learning tutor for adults. It helps you set a career or learning goal, works out the skills you need, builds a plan that fits your week, and teaches and coaches you along the way. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to iamabl.com and the ABL app (the "Service").

ABL is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS]. You can reach us about privacy at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

## 1. The short version

- We collect what you tell ABL (your goal, background, resume, answers and conversations) so we can teach and coach you.
- ABL uses AI. Your conversations and resume are processed by our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate responses. Anthropic does not use them to train its models.
- We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
- You can see, correct and delete what ABL knows about you at any time on the **What ABL knows about me** page.
- ABL is for people 18 and over.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account information:** your name, email address and profile picture, through our sign-in provider. If you sign in with Google, see section 7.
- **Your career brief:** where you are now (role, work, industry, experience, strengths) and where you want to go, plus your motivation, what success looks like to you, your deadline, how much time you have each week, when you like to learn, what you've tried before, your priorities and interests.
- **Your resume or LinkedIn profile,** if you choose to upload one during goal discovery. We use it to understand your background. We don't store the file itself; we keep the career brief and skills profile built from it (see section 5).
- **Your conversations with ABL:** goal discovery, the skills check, tutoring sessions, quick side questions ("sidekicks") and your replies to coach check-ins.
- **Your answers and work:** answers to skill checks and exercises, and anything you paste into a session, such as code or queries.
- **Corrections and settings:** changes you make to your details or skill levels, and your choices when reworking your plan.

Please don't include sensitive information that ABL doesn't need, such as health information, government ID numbers or financial account details, in your resume, brief or conversations. If you tell us about a constraint that touches on health (for example, that you have limited energy on some days), we use it only to shape your plan and you can delete it at any time.

### Information ABL creates about you

To teach you well, ABL builds a picture of your learning:

- a **skills profile** estimating your level in each skill your target role needs, and where each estimate came from;
- your **skills gap** and **learning plan**;
- **progress and mastery records**, including session summaries, homework, and a review schedule;
- **coach notes**, such as when you might benefit from a check-in or a reworked plan.

### Information collected automatically

- **Usage information:** pages you visit and features you use, collected with our product analytics provider, PostHog. It's linked to your account ID once you sign in.
- **Session replay:** PostHog records how you interact with pages (for example, clicks, scrolling and page layout) so we can find bugs and improve the design. We configure it to hide the text on the page, including your conversations, and the contents of form fields.
- **AI usage records:** for each AI request, which model was used, how many tokens it took, how long it took and what it cost. These records don't contain the content of your conversations.
- **Device and log information:** your IP address, browser type, and request logs, collected by our hosting provider when you use the Service.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see section 8.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- **Provide ABL:** understand your goal, assess your skills, build and adjust your plan, run tutoring sessions, answer side questions and send you coach check-ins in the app.
- **Personalize your learning:** use your background and interests for examples, pacing and projects.
- **Keep the Service working and secure:** authenticate you, prevent abuse, debug problems and monitor reliability and cost.
- **Improve ABL:** understand which features help people learn, using usage analytics and session replay as described above.
- **Communicate with you** about your account, changes to these terms or this policy, and, if you ask us to, reminders.
- **Meet legal obligations** and enforce our [Terms of Service](/terms).

We don't use your conversations, resume or brief to train AI models. We may review individual conversations only to investigate a problem you report, to keep the Service safe, or where the law requires it.

### How AI is used

ABL's tutor, coach, skills check and planning are powered by AI. When you use them, the relevant information (for example, your brief, skills profile and the current conversation) is sent to our AI provider to generate a response. ABL also uses AI with web search to research what employers currently ask for in your target role; those searches describe the role, market and industry, not you.

AI estimates your skill levels and builds your plan. These are learning recommendations, not decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you, and you stay in control: you can see the reasons behind each estimate, correct any level, and rework your plan at any time.

## 4. How we share your information

We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run ABL, under contracts that limit their use of it to providing their service to us:

| Provider | What it does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | AI models that power ABL | Your brief, profile, plan context and conversations, and your resume during goal discovery |
| Clerk | Sign-in and account security | Your account information and sign-in activity |
| Neon | Database hosting | Everything ABL stores about you (section 2) |
| Vercel | Website and app hosting | Requests to the Service, including IP address and request logs |
| Inngest | Background jobs (building your profile and plan, the coach, plan updates) | Job data, which can include your brief, profile and plan, kept for a limited time as job history |
| PostHog | Product analytics and session replay | Usage events linked to your account ID, and session recordings with page text hidden |

Anthropic processes content under its commercial terms: it doesn't use it to train models, and it deletes inputs and outputs within about 30 days, except where it needs to keep them longer for safety or legal reasons. If we add or change a provider that receives your personal information, we'll update this policy.

We may also share information:

- if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or ABL;
- in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information;
- with your consent.

## 5. How long we keep your information

- **While you have an account,** we keep your brief, profile, plans, sessions, progress and coach notes so ABL can keep teaching you.
- **Resume files** aren't stored by ABL. The file is sent to our AI provider during goal discovery, and we keep the brief and profile built from it.
- **Discovery conversations** aren't stored after your brief is confirmed. Tutoring session transcripts and side questions are kept with your account.
- **When you delete your data** on the What ABL knows about me page, we delete your ABL records immediately. Copies may remain in our database backups for up to [BACKUP RETENTION, e.g. 30] days before they're overwritten.
- **Analytics data** in PostHog isn't removed by in-app deletion. You can ask us to delete it (section 6).
- **Your sign-in account** with Clerk stays after you delete your ABL data, so you can start again. You can delete it too by [HOW TO DELETE THE ACCOUNT, e.g. "choosing Delete account in your account settings" or "emailing us"].
- **Aggregated information** that can't identify you may be kept longer.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **See and correct your information:** the **What ABL knows about me** page shows your brief, each skill level and the reason for it. You can edit your details and correct any level.
- **Delete your information:** you can delete everything ABL knows about you from that page.
- **Other requests:** email [PRIVACY EMAIL] to ask for a copy of your information, to delete analytics data, or to exercise any other right you have. We'll verify your request comes from your account before acting on it.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, get a copy of it, correct it, delete it, and object to or restrict some uses of it. We'll honor these requests regardless of where you live, and we won't treat you differently for making them.

**California residents:** in the last 12 months we collected the categories of information in section 2: identifiers (name, email, account ID, IP address); professional and employment-related information (your background, resume and goals); education-related information (your skills, progress and assessments); internet activity (usage and session replay); and inferences (your skill estimates and plan). We collect them for the purposes in section 3 and share them only with the service providers in section 4. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would give you the right to limit it.

**If you're in the EU, UK or EEA:** we process your information to provide the Service you signed up for (performance of a contract), for our legitimate interests in securing and improving it, and, where required, with your consent (which you can withdraw at any time). Our providers are in the United States. Where they're certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension we rely on that; otherwise we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 7. Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them only to create your account and sign you in. We don't use Google user data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it except with the service providers in section 4 as needed to run ABL.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

- **Essential:** our sign-in provider sets cookies that keep you signed in and protect your account. The Service doesn't work without them.
- **Analytics:** PostHog uses a cookie and your browser's local storage to recognize your visits and count usage.

We don't use advertising cookies. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, we turn off analytics for that browser. We don't sell or share personal information, so there's nothing for a Global Privacy Control signal to opt you out of, but we treat it as such a request.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls and reputable infrastructure providers. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.
